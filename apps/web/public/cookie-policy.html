Last updated: July 19, 2026

VetPurse ("we", "our", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember your actions and preferences over time, and can be used to recognize your device on return visits.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to personalize content and ads, to provide social media features, and to analyze our traffic. Cookies fall into the following categories:

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function — such as remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be turned off.

Analytics

Helps us understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on site, and general traffic patterns — so we can improve it.

Personalization & ads

Used to tailor content and advertising to your interests, on this site and elsewhere.

Social media

Enables content sharing and embedded features from social platforms.

Managing cookies in your browser

In addition to the controls above, most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how parts of the site function.

Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
